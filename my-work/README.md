# Your work goes here

Nothing in this folder is part of the app, and nothing here is ever uploaded
anywhere. It is just a tidy place to keep things so they are not scattered
across your Desktop and Downloads.

A layout that works:

```
my-work/
  graphs/      graphs you saved with the Export button
  images/      source images you feed into Tripo
  models/      .glb meshes you downloaded
  videos/      clips you downloaded from Runway
```

Two habits worth having:

- **Download what you want to keep.** Tripo and Runway hand back links that
  expire after a while. The file is only really yours once it is on your Mac.
- **Export a graph when you get a setup you like** (Export button, top of the
  app). It saves a small `.json` you can Import later, or on another computer.
  Your keys are never inside it.
